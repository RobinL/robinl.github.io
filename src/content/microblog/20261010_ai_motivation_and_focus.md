title: "AI, motivation and focus"
date: "2026-10-10"
tags: ["AI", "Work"]
---

AI means:

- People can build more things
- More people can build things

And yet, I don't see an explosion of projects.

Is it motivation that's in short supply?  At least for some, AI seem to undermine motivation.  And distraction is everywhere.  The ability to focus and the motivation to persistently work on things seems increasingly scarce, at just the time AI is making it more valuable.
