title: "AI progress and white collar disruption"
date: "2026-08-01"
tags: ["AI", "Work"]
---

With stories like [this](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) it's becoming increasingly clear to me that the more bullish predictions about AI progress seem the most accurate.  It now seems likely to be that AI will be able to vastly outcompete humans in most white collar fields, in the same way it's already beginning to in programming.  And whilst comparative advantage means there will always be a role for humans, I also feel like this is going to cause pretty extreme disruption in most fields where the task is synthesising information and data.
